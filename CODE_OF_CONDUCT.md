# OpenLegalCore Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want OpenLegalCore spaces to support rigorous, useful, and respectful collaboration across technical, legal, research, language, and policy backgrounds.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- critique ideas, methods, and evidence rather than people;
- distinguish verified facts from assumptions and opinions;
- acknowledge uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest;
- protect privacy, confidentiality, security, and intellectual property;
- help keep discussions focused and accessible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing confidential information, impersonation, spam, and knowingly misleading claims.

## Scope

This code applies in OpenLegalCore repositories, Discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces, as well as when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, remove, lock, or close content; restrict participation; or take other proportionate action when this code is violated. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from OpenLegalCore spaces.

Do not report conduct concerns publicly when doing so could expose a person or sensitive information. Use a private contact route published by OpenLegalCore or contact an organization owner through an existing private collaboration channel.

Enforcement decisions are made by the project leadership. Good-faith disagreement with a technical or governance decision is not, by itself, a conduct violation.
