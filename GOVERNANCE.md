# OpenLegalCore Community Governance

This document summarizes how the public OpenLegalCore community operates. The
current public governance record, including named decision rights and their
boundaries, is maintained at
[openlegalcore.org/governance](https://openlegalcore.org/governance/).

## Operating model

OpenLegalCore uses a **public foundation + private incubation** model.

The public foundation contains useful, documented and reviewable components and
project records that are safe to publish. Immature, security-sensitive,
commercially sensitive or confidential work may remain in private incubation.
A documented component is not thereby public, released or available.

## Current authority

OpenLegalCore is founder-led and publicly accountable. **Rajko Majcen, Founder /
Product Lead**, currently carries final project-wide responsibility. Public
discussion provides reviewable input; it does not make responsibility anonymous
or transfer a decision right.

The six current decision areas are:

1. **Product direction** — direction and the order in which project work is
   pursued.
2. **Component boundaries** — component scope, access state and readiness gate.
3. **Release and publication** — releases, evidence publication and other public
   status changes.
4. **Maintainer and governance appointments** — maintainer appointments and
   changes to the decision-rights boundary.
5. **Private development access** — access to private code, documentation,
   evidence artefacts and credentials.
6. **Brand and identity** — the OpenLegalCore name, visual identity and public
   project representation.

Responsibility may be delegated expressly to maintainers or reviewers. Funding,
a contribution or access to private work does not itself create governance,
ownership or maintainer rights.

## Roles

- **Founder / Product Lead:** holds the named project-wide decision rights.
- **Maintainer:** stewards an expressly assigned area after explicit
  appointment.
- **Contributor:** contributes without standing management rights.
- **Reviewer or domain expert:** provides technical, legal, language, security,
  research or user-experience review.
- **Sponsor or partner:** provides scoped financial, material or expert support
  without automatic governance or access rights.

Maintainer status follows sustained contribution, sound judgement, trusted
review and explicit appointment. It is not automatic.

## Significant decisions

A significant public decision should record:

1. the problem;
2. verified context;
3. the chosen approach;
4. serious alternatives;
5. consequences and risks;
6. status;
7. responsible person and date.

Sensitive security, privacy, incident, contractual, production-data and
architecture matters may be handled privately. When safe and useful, the public
effect of a private decision should later be summarized without exposing
protected information.

## Separate rights

Code, data, documentation and brand rights remain separate. A public artefact
opens only the rights expressly granted by its own licence or publication
record. No project-wide licence is inferred from one released component.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes may be proposed publicly in the **Governance and method**
discussion category. They take effect only after an explicit decision by the
current authority and an update to the durable public record.
