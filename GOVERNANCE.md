# OpenLegalCore Community Governance

## Operating model

OpenLegalCore uses a **public foundation + private incubation** model.

The public foundation contains useful, documented, and reviewable components and project records that are safe to publish. Immature, security-sensitive, commercially sensitive, or confidential work may remain in private incubation.

## Decision model

OpenLegalCore is founder-led. Public discussion is encouraged and substantive contributions are evaluated on quality, correctness, safety, evidence, and maintainability. Discussion is not a plebiscite, and funding does not purchase governance or maintainer rights.

The project lead is accountable for final project-wide decisions and may delegate responsibility to maintainers or reviewers.

## Roles

- **Project lead:** sets direction and makes final project-wide decisions.
- **Maintainer:** reviews contributions and stewards an assigned area.
- **Contributor:** contributes without standing management rights.
- **Reviewer or domain expert:** provides technical, legal, language, security, research, or user-experience review.
- **Sponsor or partner:** provides financial, material, or expert support without automatic governance rights.

## Significant decisions

A significant public decision should record:

1. the problem;
2. verified context;
3. the chosen approach;
4. serious alternatives;
5. consequences and risks;
6. status;
7. responsible person and date.

Sensitive security, privacy, incident, contractual, production-data, and architecture matters may be handled privately. When safe and useful, the public effect of a private decision should later be summarized without exposing protected information.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes should be proposed publicly in the **Governance and method** discussion category and recorded in this repository when adopted.
