# Security and Sensitive Information

Do not report vulnerabilities, active incidents, credentials, private URLs, deployment details, private legal documents, personal data, or other sensitive information in public issues or Discussions.

## Reporting route

1. If the matter affects a specific public repository, use that repository's **Security** tab and private vulnerability reporting when available.
2. Otherwise, contact an OpenLegalCore organization owner through an existing private collaboration channel.
3. Share only the minimum information needed to establish a safe private reporting route. Do not attach confidential documents until the recipient and handling method are confirmed.

If no private route is available, do not open a public report. A general, non-sensitive request for private contact may be posted without technical details.

## What to include privately

- the affected component and version or commit;
- a concise description of the impact;
- reproducible steps using synthetic data where possible;
- relevant logs or evidence with secrets and personal data removed;
- any known mitigation.

OpenLegalCore will acknowledge a valid private report when operationally possible, assess scope and severity, and coordinate disclosure or remediation as appropriate. This document does not create a service-level agreement or guarantee a specific response time.
